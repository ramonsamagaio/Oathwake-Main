# Dylearn-style Grass Only Lab

Preferred scene:

`res://scenes/labs/DylearnStyleGrassOnlyLab.tscn`

This is an isolated visual/performance experiment for Oathwake grass. It deliberately does not use AuthoringLab, the authored grass/dirt TileSet, existing ground sprites, or the normal Player sprite. Procedural dirt remains out of scope until the grass target is approved.

## Reference

The architecture is informed by Dylearn's public `Dylearn-3D-Pixel-Art-Grass-Demo`, especially its separation between floor heterogeneity, instanced grass sprites, accent leaves, world-space moving wind noise, stepped animation, fake perspective, and character-reactive displacement.

Dylearn's code is MIT licensed and its reusable art assets are CC BY 4.0. This lab does not copy the reference grass images. Its six 16x16 silhouettes are embedded and generated by Oathwake at runtime.

## V3 goals

V3 keeps the successful grass-only direction while making it less visually noisy and substantially cheaper to render.

### Visual pass

- Four common grass silhouettes plus two rarer accent silhouettes are packed into one runtime-generated 96x16 atlas.
- Accent frequency is intentionally low so bright shapes stop looking evenly sprinkled across the whole field.
- The palette is more muted moss/olive and closer to Oathwake's current terrain language.
- Floor colour is organized into broader world-space patches instead of dense micro-speckle.
- Grass density is lower, but each tuft is slightly larger and visually fuller.
- The root remains planted while an analytic top-weighted bend mask makes upper pixels respond more strongly.

### Wind pass

- A seamless 128x128 wind noise texture is generated once from `FastNoiseLite` at runtime.
- The shader samples that texture twice with slightly diverging directions and scales, following the useful part of the reference approach.
- The combined signal becomes a travelling gust mask. Strong motion crosses the field in coherent bands instead of moving every tuft equally.
- Per-instance susceptibility makes some grass nearly static even when a weak gust passes over it.
- Animation remains quantised, with near/mid/far LOD materials using progressively lower stepped rates.

### Performance pass

- The old procedural hash/value-noise/FBM loops were removed from both grass and ground shaders.
- A seamless 256x256 patch texture is generated once and reused by the ground and grass materials.
- Base spacing changed from 14x11 to 17x14 pixels, cutting the full-field candidate count by roughly one third before culling.
- Chunks are smaller and culled against the current camera viewport plus a safety margin.
- Near chunks draw all instances with full wind, fake perspective, and player displacement.
- Mid chunks draw a deterministic 72% subset with weaker/slower wind and no player displacement.
- Far visible chunks draw a deterministic 46% subset with minimal/slower wind and no player displacement.
- Entries are deterministically shuffled inside each chunk before applying `visible_instance_count`, so LOD thinning stays spatially distributed.
- Player displacement uniforms update at 20 Hz and chunk LOD updates at about 5.5 Hz instead of doing CPU-side management every rendered frame.

## Runtime-generated resources

No grass PNG import is required for this lab.

At runtime `DylearnStyleGrassOnlyField2D.gd` creates:

- one 96x16 RGBA grass atlas from six embedded pixel masks;
- one 256x256 seamless colour-patch noise texture;
- one 128x128 seamless wind noise texture;
- three shared shader materials for near, mid, and far grass LODs.

## Test

1. Checkout/pull `feature/procedural-terrain-grass-v1`.
2. Open `res://scenes/labs/DylearnStyleGrassOnlyLab.tscn`.
3. Run the current scene with F6.
4. Move with WASD/arrows and hold Shift to run.
5. Look for broad floor colour masses rather than granular static.
6. Watch the wind travel across the field in irregular bands rather than synchronizing all grass.
7. Verify nearby grass still reacts to the test walker.
8. Compare frame pacing/GPU load against the V2 commit if needed.

## Next after visual approval

Only after the grass-only target is approved should procedural dirt be added. Dirt should consume the same deterministic world-space sampler rather than reintroducing a separate random system.
